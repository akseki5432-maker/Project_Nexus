Words to know
Free cash flow
Cash left each year after paying costs, taxes and new equipment. It's what the owners and lenders could take out.
Discount rate
The yearly return you need. $110 next year is worth $100 today if you could earn 10% elsewhere.
Terminal value
The value of all the years after the forecast, assuming slow growth forever. It's often most of the value, which is why it's worth questioning.
Whole business value
Also called enterprise value: what the business is worth to its owners and lenders together, like a house's full price before the mortgage.
Equity value
What's left for the owners after debts are paid off. Divide by the number of shares to get the value of one share.
Depreciation
Spreading the cost of equipment over the years it lasts. It counts against profit but isn't cash leaving, so it's added back.

Words to know
NPV (net present value)
All the project's future cash in today's dollars, minus what it costs to start. Above zero means it beats your required return.
Monte Carlo simulation
Running the numbers thousands of times with random guesses, like rolling dice over and over to learn the odds.
Standard deviation
How spread out the results are. A bigger number means outcomes vary more.
Likely range
The middle 90% of outcomes. Only 1 future in 20 was worse than the low end, and 1 in 20 better than the high end.

How many units to break even?

Every sale earns a little toward your fixed bills like rent. Break-even is the number of sales where money in equals money out.

Unit economics
Bills that stay the same however much you sell, like rent and salaries.

Words to know
Fixed costs
Costs that don't change with sales, like rent.
Variable costs
Costs for each unit made, like ingredients.
Contribution margin
Price minus the cost to make one unit: what each sale adds toward fixed costs and then profit.
Margin of safety
How far sales could fall before you start losing money.
Operating leverage
With high fixed costs, profit swings more than sales. A 3.5x swing means 10% more sales gives about 35% more profit.
